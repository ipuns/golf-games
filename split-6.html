How points are split
  • Lowest score: 4 points. Middle: 2. Highest: 0.
  • Two tie for lowest: 3 points each, the other gets 0.
  • Two tie for highest: the low scorer gets 4, the other two get 1 each.
  • All three tie: 2 points each.
