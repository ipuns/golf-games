How points are split (3 players)
  • Solo player wins the hole: 6 points.
  • The two-player team wins the hole: 3 points each.
  • Tie: 2 points each.
  • Partnering with someone doesn't protect the Wolf from a solo opponent — it decides who that opponent is.
How points are split (4 players)
  • Partner picked, that team wins: 2 points each to the Wolf and partner.
  • Partner picked, the other team wins: 3 points each to the other two.
  • Lone Wolf wins: 4 points to the Wolf.
  • Lone Wolf loses: 1 point each to the other three.
  • Tie: no points, but the Wolf role still moves on.
